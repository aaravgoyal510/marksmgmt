The Internal Assessment (IA) & Attendance Portal is an institute-grade web application built for Bharati Vidyapeeth Institute of Management and Research (BVIMR). The core architectural tenet of the system is a **pure configuration-driven calculation engine**: grading rules, CES component counts, maximum marks, attendance slab conversion curves, and relative weightages are defined per subject offering by the Head of Department (HOD) rather than hardcoded into application code ([TechSpec.md:19-22](file:///C:/Users/admin/Desktop/internal marks calculated/TechSpec.md#L19-L22), [backend/src/services/gradingEngine.ts:41-96](file:///C:/Users/admin/Desktop/internal marks calculated/backend/src/services/gradingEngine.ts#L41-L96)).

## Conventions

- **Naming Conventions:**
  - Route handlers & controllers: camelCase verbs (`createDepartment`, `upsertGradingPolicy`, `bulkUpsertMarks`).
  - Routes: kebab-case REST nouns (`/api/subject-offerings/:offeringId/policy/duplicate-last-term`).
  - Prisma Models: PascalCase nouns (`StudentProfile`, `GradingPolicy`, `SubjectOfferingSection`).
  - Database Fields: camelCase mapped directly in Prisma (`confirmedAt`, `cesGroupWeightage`).
  - Enums: UPPER_SNAKE_CASE (`Role.FACULTY`, `ScoreGroup.CES`, `QueryStatus.ESCALATED`).

- **Security & Authorization Conventions:**
